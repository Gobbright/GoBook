   "Deleted Users"

   "Expired Users"

4. DATA DISPLAY STRUCTURE:

   Each user row shows:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone
   - Business Name
   - Category (retail, manufacturing, etc.)
   - Subscription Plan (blank for trial users)
   - Subscription Amount (0 for trial users)
   - Status (Active, Blocked, Deleted, Expired)
   - Created Date
   - Last Login

5. HOW TRIAL USERS ARE IDENTIFIED:

   Backend:
   - subscriptionPlan === "" (empty string)
   - subscriptionAmount === 0

   Frontend:
   - isTrialUser(row) function checks: !row.subscriptionPlan || row.subscriptionPlan === ''
   - getRowHighlightClass(row) applies amber styling to trial users
